# 1 ACROSS - SAMI
Assistant has French friends, but Sweden's at the top (4)

AMIS ("friends" in French), moving S (Sweden) to the first postion gives SAMI.
The titular robotic assistant from SAMI.

# 3 ACROSS - TREEFOLKEN
Elk toe smashes into fern, revealing brambly people (10)

ELK TOE and FERN anagrammed ("smashed into" each other) to give TREEFOLKEN, a
group of "brambly people." From The Greencloak Wanderer, the Treefolken are a
group of people who live at the top of a big tree and have plants growing from
them.

# 9 ACROSS - PUNNY
Newton hugged by wimpy Maxx, maybe (5)

N (Newton) inserted into PUNY (wimpy substitution) as a way to describe Maxx,
who often speaks in puns.

# 10 ACROSS - MAPLE LEAF
Jesse's home symbol let loose pale flame (5,4)

PALE FLAME anagrammed (let loose) to form MAPLE LEAF, the symbol of Canada
(where Jesse's from)

# 12 ACROSS - INHIBITOR
Some idiots now have irrecoverable bopyrid infection that orchestrates real
hindrance (9)

First letters (some) of "**I**diots ... **R**eal" gives INHIBITOR meaning
"hindrance." Bopyrids are a parasite in Sludge-Swimmer.

# 13 ACROSS - NAIAD
Nymph's new artificial intelligences are dead (5)

N (new) + AI (artificial intelligences) + A (are) + D (dead) = NAIAD, a type of
nymph. Naiads feature prominently in Legend of Maxx.

# 14 ACROSS - SMOOTHER
Nicer ale is more suave (8)

Double definition of "Nicer ale" (SMOOTHER in terms of beverage quality) and
"more suave" (SMOOTHER in terms of social sophistication). Only tangentially
tied to Legend of Maxx through the word "ale".

# 15 ACROSS - ANGEL
Remmy's bizarre lineage has no one in it (5)

"has no one in it" is an indicator to delete the I from LINEAGE, anagrammed with
"bizarre" to form ANGEL. Definition is "Remmy," an angel from Legend of Maxx.

# 17 ACROSS - END IT
Mero's objective is hidden expenditures? (3,2)

END IT is hidden in exp**ENDIT**ures. Definition of "Mero's objective" as his
whole goal in the book is to die.

# 19 ACROSS - SEA SIREN
On a regular basis in Georgia state is a northbound ship (3,5)

Alternating letters of "i**N** g**E**o**R**g**I**a **S**t**A**e**E** i**S**",
reversed, giving the name of the ship from the simulation in SAMI.

# 22 ACROSS - CYRIL
Drunkenly announced breakfast guide (5)

Homophone indicator, "cereal" (breakfast) can sound like "Cyril" if said in
slurred speech (drunkenly announced). Definition of "guide" from Legend of Maxx
where Cyril is the name of the guide.

# 24 ACROSS - PAGAN
Heretic is accepted amidst tumultuous pang (5)

A ("accepted" substitution) contained in PAGN ("pang" anagram, "tumultuous"
anagrind) for a heretic. "Heretic" is the anthology book that Sludge-Swimmer was
published in.

# 25 ACROSS - FEY
In the end, staff blame crazy elves, perhaps (3)

Ends of "staf**F** blam**E** craz**Y**" give FEY, a category of creature to
which elves belong (definition by example with "perhaps"). From The Greencloak
Wanderer.

# 26 ACROSS - AKA
Handle grasped by Greencloak adventurer? (3)

AKA hidden in (grasped by) Greenclo**AK A**dventurer. "Handle" as definition for
"AKA" is a bit stretchy, hence the "?"

# 27 ACROSS - DEPTH
Bottom of first hellhole found below department (5)

H (first hellhole) after (below in down clue) DEPT (department) gives DEPTH,
meaning bottom. Hellhole from Mero and the City of Ghosts.

# 28 ACROSS - TABLE
Bill starts to leave early for bed (5)

TAB (substitution for bill, like at a bar) + L E (acrostic of Leave Early).
Definition of "bed" comes from the Legion of Maxx.

# 29 ACROSS - TOM COLLINS
Clint looms, floundering for a drink (3,7)

Anagram of (CLINT LOOMS)* with an anagrind of "floundering". Tom Collins is a
drink, notably favored by June in the Legend of Maxx.

# 30 ACROSS - AEIN
Thousands elude ageing girl (4)

AGEING - Gs (thousands) = AEIN, definition "girl". Aein is the main character in
The Greencloak Wanderer.
