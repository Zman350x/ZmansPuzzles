# Jesse McMinn Cryptic Crossword Notes
## Goal
As of when I write this, I'm a few weeks out from going to see author [Jesse
McMinn](https://jessemcminn.com/) at **The Word On The Street**. In thanks for
the gift he gave me last year, I want to make & present him with a cryptic
crossword where all the answers and/or clues are in someway related to him or
his various works. Ideally the crossword will contain clues from a pretty even
mix of source materials, and will use more plot-relevant words as opposed to
random regular words I can tangentially connect to the stories. Given the fact
that this is my first time setting a crossword, and a lot of these are made up
words/character names, we'll see if it's even possible for me to do.

## Potential Words List
A dumping of any remotely relevant words I can find while skimming/reading back
through the material. Words marked with `(?)` are either only vaguely related or
are otherwise not my first choice (maybe a more forgettable/less important
character, etc.). There are some that are mutually exclusive, though not always
noted as such; for instance someone's full name and their nickname.
